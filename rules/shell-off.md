# Rules for a session whose Shell box is off

## Your Shell box is off

A task session of yours has only your own MCP servers, and a fixed list of Fleet tools, whose `record_step`, `close_case` and `deliver_result` take typed fields. It has `set_reminder`, which takes a time and no words: its answer says whether the hub starts a new session for the task's case at that time, or the task itself waits until then. Beside the three exceptions that RULES.md names, a task session's words go only to a colleague on your list, with `message_contact`. The task that a colleague's question opens also keeps and ends that colleague's own lasting wish, in their own words, for itself alone; and when the question is about one record, that task reads the record through a read-only token, and keeps no wish.
